## [How to Better Communicate as a Data Scientist](https://www.divingintodata.com/p/how-to-better-communicate-as-a-data-scientist-6fc5428d3143)
## [The Most Undervalued Skill for Data Scientists](https://towardsdatascience.com/the-most-undervalued-skill-for-data-scientists-e0e0d7709321)

I'll channel the message of these two articles (they reference one another and are by the same author) by getting straight to the point: Clear communication - that is, writing - is the final step to getting any good data analysis across the finish line. 

I'm going to attempt re-building a Google Slides presentation with some of the tips from this article; previous attempts at cleaning it up have felt difficult without a direct audience, strucutre, or really, a story. So often you'll be underwater, diving to the depths of an analysis, that once you finally come up to the surface you no longer remember how to breath air. This presentation in particular failed to pass muster a couple of times, and granted the audience changed a few times as well which didn't help, but as this article points out, a good presentation is flexible enough to allow exec audiences to skip the details and focus on the key takeaways.
